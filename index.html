About us

Thirteen years of getting up at four

Bakerz Bite started with one second-hand oven and a stubborn belief that bread should taste of something. We still mix every dough by hand, still use real butter, and still bake everything on the day you buy it.

  • Slow-proved dough, never rushed with additives
  • Butter, cream and eggs from farms we know by name
  • Whatever is unsold at closing goes to the local shelter

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Tap a question to read the answer.

Do you take custom cake orders?

Yes! Celebration cakes are made to order with 48 hours' notice. Pop into the shop or use the contact form below to tell us the date, the number of people and any flavours to avoid.

Do you deliver?

Free local delivery on orders over $40, within a 5km radius. Larger orders can be arranged by phone. Everything else is pick-up from the shop on Maple Street.

Are your products suitable for vegans?

We bake a daily vegan sourdough and a vegan oat cookie. Most of our cakes and pastries use butter and free-range eggs. Ask at the counter and we will tell you exactly what is in each bake.

Can I freeze your bread?

Yes - sourdough freezes beautifully. Slice it first, then toast straight from frozen. Pastries and cakes are best eaten fresh, within two days of buying.

Do you offer baking classes?

Yes, on the first Saturday of every month. A small group, three hours, and you take home everything you bake. Book through the contact form or at the counter.

What time do you open?

Monday to Friday 7am-6pm, Saturday 7:30am-5pm, Sunday 8am-2pm. The oven goes on at 4am, so mornings are always the best time for the freshest bakes.
